## Acceptance and implementation

- Accepted readings survive unchanged: the page uses the shared scorer observation selector with the supplied clock and accepted date-only snapshot anchor. The chart no longer retimes snapshots with `created_at` or censors a fast launch rise through a second stale-value heuristic.
- Estimated launch history starts at an explicitly assumed zero, rises without exceeding the first observation, and meets that observation exactly. Later high readings cannot lift earlier history over that boundary. A `P0D` live/premiere placeholder opts out of an invented origin; archived live timing without a distinct launch timestamp remains an assumed publish origin, labeled as such.
- Long gaps stay between their two observed endpoint counts. Genuine observed corrections remain intact. Intermediate points disclose interpolation; only actual readings get observation dots.
- Neither the main plot nor overview borrows estimated points into the solid measured path. Estimated paths may borrow the real endpoint for a continuous join. Separate estimated gaps no longer connect across measured intervals.
- V5 retains its stored same-age multiplier past day 30. The score marker connects its stored count to its exact stored typical count at the scored age. When the header shows a different count, the comparison discloses which count it used.
- The old `C(30) × growth shape` typical line is omitted for V5. It is not V5's weighted C(t). A complete C(t) line remains dependent on frozen prior/parameter-fit context; forcing a curve through today's stored denominator would conceal the disagreement elsewhere.
- No default forecast ribbons. Legacy day-30 tables and trajectory-width heuristics do not establish exact-horizon coverage. Projection labels and tooltips say tentative. `forecastBandAt` is the narrow future integration seam for a validated exact-age/exact-horizon interval.
- A stored day-30 estimate is not silently moved onto a newer chart observation. Until rescored, the chart uses the tentative growth shape from the latest accepted observation. Forecast accuracy/calibration is the separate scoring-evaluation workstream.

Range chips, brush pointer/keyboard operation, log scale, packaging marks and hover context remain in place. No broad visual redesign. On small screens, tracking and last-observation labels wrap above the plot rather than overlapping each other or a title-change mark.
